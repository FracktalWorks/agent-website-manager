Capabilities

Six processes. One facility.

Every technology we offer runs on machines we built ourselves or validated in our own service bureau. No outsourcing — your parts stay under our quality system from start to finish.

FDM

Fused Deposition Modeling

Production-grade thermoplastics — ABS, PC, Nylon, CF-Nylon, ULTEM. Build volume up to 600×600×600 mm. Ideal for functional prototypes, jigs, fixtures, and end-use parts.

Learn More
SLS

Selective Laser Sintering

Production-grade nylon parts on the Apollo 350 — CO₂ laser, 350×350×420 mm build. No supports needed. Complex geometries, living hinges, and functional assemblies in PA12 and PA11.

Learn More
SLA

Stereolithography

High-resolution photopolymer parts with surface finish comparable to injection moulding. Tolerances within ±0.1 mm. Ideal for visual prototypes, master patterns, and dental models.

Learn More
MJF

Multi Jet Fusion

High-productivity nylon printing with isotropic mechanical properties. Faster than SLS for batch production. Consistent part quality across the entire build volume.

Learn More
Casting

Vacuum Casting

Polyurethane parts from silicone moulds — up to 30 copies per mould. Wide range of shore hardness and colours. The right choice for low-volume production before committing to tooling.
