Strategy

Positioning: the vitiligo wedge

With 119 dermatologists listed in Ameerpet alone and aggregators (Practo, Justdial, Apollo 24/7) owning the generic SERPs, competing head-on for "dermatologist in hyderabad" is a long war. The winning sequence is: dominate the specialty, then widen.

  • 1. Own vitiligo & psoriasis — the clinic is literally named for it, competition is thin, India is the world's largest vitiligo market, and patients travel across states for a trusted specialist. Practo already carries repigmentation success stories — surface them. Add teleconsult + a "WhatsApp your photos" flow and the catchment becomes national.
  • 2. Ride the doctor's credential — Dr. (Lt Col) Khagendra Varada: 41 years of practice, MBBS (Osmania, 1981), Dip. Practical Dermatology (Cardiff, UK), Dip. Dermatology (AAAM), IMA member, 97% patient recommendation and 61 verified Practo stories. That's E-E-A-T gold — most competitor clinics publish anonymous content.
  • 3. Then harvest volume — hair fall, acne, allergy and the aesthetics menu (botox, boosters, RF, peels) once the domain has specialty authority and reviews compound on the Business Profile.

Conversion

Practo-first funnel & landing-page anatomy

The clinic team runs on Practo today, so every CTA on the site terminates in a Practo booking — the website's job is to win the search click before Practo's own pages do, then hand the patient to the familiar workflow. This matters more than it sounds: when patients land directly on Practo search, Dr. Varada is displayed beside 119 competing dermatologists; when they land on drvarada.com first, the booking link goes only to him.

Execution

90-day plan

  1. Weeks 1–2 · Foundation. Untangle the entity: verify which locations are active, align Google Business Profile(s) with the Practo listings (categories "Dermatologist" + "Skin care clinic" + "Hair transplantation clinic", link drvarada.com, photos); replace placeholder pages; publish Home, Doctor profile, both Location pages and Contact with Practo booking links; schema markup.

Claim/align Practo, Justdial, Apollo 24/7, Skedoc, ClinicSpots listings (identical NAP); local citations; consider ₹15–25K/mo Google Ads on "vitiligo treatment hyderabad" + "botox hyderabad" while organic ranks build; install call/WhatsApp tracking to measure per-page leads.
