Questions

Before you come in

Can vitiligo be cured?

No treatment cures vitiligo. What treatment can do — and often does — is repigment affected skin and stop the spread. How much repigments depends on the site, how long the patch has been there, and whether the disease is stable. You'll be told which of those apply to you at the first visit.

How long before I see a change?

For chronic conditions, months rather than weeks, and the first review is usually at six to eight weeks. Anything promising faster than that is promising something it cannot deliver.

Do I need a referral?

No. Book directly on Practo and bring whatever prescriptions or photographs you already have.

Do you treat patients from outside Hyderabad?

Yes — particularly for vitiligo surgery, where patients travel from across Telangana and beyond. Write with photographs before travelling so we can tell you whether the procedure is appropriate for your case.
