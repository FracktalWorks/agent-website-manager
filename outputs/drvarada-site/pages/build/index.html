Questions

Before you book.

Can vitiligo be cured?

There is no treatment that guarantees a cure. What good treatment can do is stabilise the condition — stop new patches appearing — and encourage repigmentation in many patients, sometimes substantially. How much depends on where the patches are, how long they've been there, and whether the vitiligo is currently active. You'll be told which category you fall into at the first consultation.

How long does repigmentation take?

Months, not weeks. Phototherapy protocols are usually reviewed at 8–12 week intervals, and facial and trunk patches typically respond better than hands and feet. Consistency matters more than intensity.

Do you offer online consultations?

Yes. Dr. Varada consults on video through Practo, which suits patients outside Hyderabad and follow-up reviews. You can send photographs on WhatsApp first if you'd like to check whether an online consultation is appropriate for your case.

What does a consultation cost?

₹500 at both clinics. Any tests or procedures are explained and priced before they're carried out.

Which clinic should I book?

Jubilee Hills (1:30–8 pm) for aesthetics, hair and general consultations. West Marredpally (7–9 am) suits early-morning medical dermatology visits and vitiligo or psoriasis reviews. Either is fine if you're unsure — book the time that works for you.

Do you treat children?

Yes. Childhood eczema, vitiligo and skin infections are common in the practice, and treatment plans are adjusted for age.
