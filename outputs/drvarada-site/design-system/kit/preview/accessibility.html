--ink-3 is the one token that does not clear AA at body size. It is for eyebrows, captions and metadata — set at 12–14 px semibold or uppercase, never for a sentence someone has to read.

Focus & motion

Every interactive element takes a visible focus ring: 2px solid var(--mint-dp) at 3px offset. The ticker, the canvas field and every transition stop under prefers-reduced-motion.

Tap targets are 44 px minimum — which is why --controlHeight is 44 and buttons pad to 15 px vertical. Patients using this site are frequently older.
