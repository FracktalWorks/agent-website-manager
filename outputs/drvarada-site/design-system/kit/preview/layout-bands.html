The page is light-committed. Healthcare and skincare audiences expect light grounds, and clinical before/after photography only reads truthfully on light. Dark is punctuation, not a theme: three per page is the ceiling.

Never place two dark bands adjacent, and never open a page on a dark band other than the utility bar.
