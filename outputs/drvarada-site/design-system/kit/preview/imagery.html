Imagery

What may be photographed, and what must be true about it.

Clinical photography

Required

Written patient consent, on file, before any before/after image is published.

Required

Same lighting, same distance, same angle. A flattering angle is a misleading result.

Required

State the interval and that results vary. Never imply a typical outcome.

This is the reason the site is light-committed. Clinical photography only reads truthfully on a light ground; a dark surround shifts perceived skin tone and quietly flatters the result.

Portraits & clinic

Natural light, real rooms, no stock. The doctor photographed at eye level, unhurried. If an asset does not exist yet, ship a labelled placeholder rather than a stock substitute — a stock clinic reads as a stock clinic.
