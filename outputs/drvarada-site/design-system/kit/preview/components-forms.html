Entry points

How a patient starts — and why there are no forms.

Symptom finder

White patches on skin →Red, scaly, flaking patches →Hair falling more than usual →Patchy bald spots →Fine lines & laxity →Unwanted hair →

Chips, not a dropdown. A patient recognises “patchy white skin” and does not yet recognise “vitiligo” — so the entry point is written in their words and the dot carries the category colour.

Inputs

The site collects no patient data. There are no booking forms, no contact forms and no newsletter capture: booking is Practo, and everything else is a phone number. That is a deliberate reduction in liability as much as in friction — health data collected on a marketing site is a burden nobody needs.

If a form is ever genuinely required, it gets its own review. Do not add one to a page because a template has a slot for it.
