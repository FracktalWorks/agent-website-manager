Misuse

What not to do, and the file to reach for instead.

NeverWhy
Don't Re-set the typeThe outlines exist precisely so nobody needs the fonts. Live text renders differently on every machine.
Don't Restack or re-space the linesSix forms already exist. Use the one that fits the space.
Don't Recolour outside the six colourwaysIncluding tinting the symbol to match a campaign.
Don't Stretch or squashAlways scale both dimensions together.
Don't Put -light on a dark groundThere is a -dark file for that, and it is a re-step rather than an inversion.
Don't Add shadows, outlines, gradients or bevelsThe mark carries its own depth through the cell ramp.
Don't Use a lockup as an avatarIt becomes illegible in a circular crop. The icon ladder exists for this.
Don't Rotate, or set on a busy photographIf a photo is unavoidable, use -mono-light over a darkened area.

The one rule that catches most mistakes: if you are about to modify the artwork, the answer is a different file, not an edit. Forty-two permutations exist so nobody has to improvise.
