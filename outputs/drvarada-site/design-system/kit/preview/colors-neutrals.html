On dark bands

On dark--on-darkBody text on a dark band
On dark strong--on-dark-strHeadings on a dark band
Dark line--dark-lineHairlines on dark

Navy versus ink is deliberate. --navy #1E3A63 is the identity colour and belongs to the logo, the icon tiles and anything printed. --ink #12202F is the interface colour: text, dark bands, footer. They are close on purpose and do different jobs — do not unify them.
