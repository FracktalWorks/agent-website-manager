# Citation audit — drvarada.com

Read from the live web on **2026-08-07** by driving a real browser (these sites
block plain fetches). Each row records what the directory says TODAY, not what
it should say.

## The headline

**Six independent citations place this practice in Ameerpet, Hyderabad 500016
— 5.7 km from the clinic.** That is the former address. Every one of them
disagrees with the Business Profile and the site, which say Karkhana,
Secunderabad 500009.

This is not cosmetic. Google corroborates a business's address by cross-checking
independent sources. Six of them saying Ameerpet against a listing saying
Karkhana is a direct contradiction on the single field local ranking depends on
most — and it is a plausible part of why the practice ranks below its own
listing. It also sends real patients 5.7 km wrong.

Correcting these is higher-value than acquiring any new citation, and it is the
work to do FIRST. A new citation added while six stale ones contradict it just
adds a seventh voice to an argument.

## Order of work

1. **Apple Business Connect** — claim and correct. It owns Maps on every
   iPhone, no Google equivalent covers that surface, and it is currently 5.7 km
   wrong.
2. **Practo** — the strongest doctor citation, pointing at another clinic.
3. **grotal, yappe, doctorsfinder, typeindia, medindia** — each has a "claim
   this listing" or "report an error" route. Free.
4. **Justdial and cybo** — verify the detail once the above are done.

Then, and only then, add new citations. Existing wrong ones outrank new right
ones for damage.

## Then: links, which are a different job

Citations corroborate; links give Googlebot a path. The crawl data on
2026-08-07 (11 indexed of 61, all of them hand-requested, none organic) says
the site has no inbound links worth the name. Best available, all legitimately
earned:

- **IADVL** — Indian Association of Dermatologists, Venereologists &
  Leprologists. The specialty body; the most topically relevant link a
  dermatologist can hold.
- **IMA** — already asserted as a membership in the site's schema.
- **Telangana State Medical Council** public register — registration 10461.
  ⚠ see the council-name conflict in [[drvarada-clinic-context]].
- **Osmania Medical College** alumni; **Cardiff University** Diploma in
  Practical Dermatology.
- Army Medical Corps veteran associations — 25 years, verifiable.
- The **46 commissioned illustrations**, offered to patient-education sites and
  vitiligo support groups under an attribution licence. **World Vitiligo Day is
  25 June**, when Indian health press reliably runs vitiligo stories.

## Never

Bought links, "link building packages", or bulk directory-submission services.
Medicine is the most scrutinised category Google has — and bulk submission
manufactures exactly the inconsistent-NAP problem this document exists to fix.
