### Photos

The largest single gap. The profile and the site's schema both have one photograph between them — the doctor's portrait. Photo count and recency are a documented local ranking input, and listings with none lose the click even when they rank.

Needed, in priority order:

1. **Exterior** — the signage, shot from the road, so an arriving patient recognises the building. Google matches this against Street View.
2. **Interior** — reception and a consulting room.
3. **At work** — the phototherapy chamber and the excimer laser; they distinguish this practice from a general clinic.
4. **Team** — the doctor, captioned by name.
5. **Logo** (circle-safe) and a **16:9 cover**.

Anything showing a patient needs consent on file before upload — the same gate the site's build applies to result imagery.
