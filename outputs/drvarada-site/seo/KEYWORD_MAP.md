## Rules

1. **One page per head term.** If two pages could rank for the same query, merge or re-point one.
2. **City pages carry the city; national hubs never do.** `/vitiligo/` and
   `/vitiligo-treatment-hyderabad/` must differ in intent — national = education & online consult,
   local = book this clinic. Cross-link them explicitly.
3. **Title tags ≤ 60 chars where possible**, primary keyword first, brand last.
4. **Every page:** one H1 matching the primary target, question-shaped H2s, FAQ block with
   FAQPage schema, internal links up to the pillar and across to siblings.
5. **No superlatives in title tags** ("best" is fine to *rank for*, not to *claim*) — NMC
   advertising ethics.
6. **Track weekly:** position for every "primary target" cell above, split by device and by
   Hyderabad vs India location.
