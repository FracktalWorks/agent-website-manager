§0

Mission — what "dominate" means, measurably

Front A · Hyderabad

Own the city for clinical services

Top-3 local pack for both locations on service + "near me" queries · top-3 organic for every {service} in hyderabad query · more Google Business Profile actions per month than any Ameerpet / Jubilee Hills competitor.

Front B · Pan-India

The vitiligo & psoriasis authority

Top-3 organic India-wide for vitiligo and psoriasis treatment head terms · the education hub patients and press cite · a steady national teleconsult + travel-patient pipeline. India has the world's highest vitiligo prevalence — the flagship deserves a national stage.

Capture model from the research phase: 40–300 incremental patients/month at 12 months. Front B raises that ceiling — chronic-condition patients travel and consult online.

§1 · Prerequisite

One entity, one story

Nothing compounds while the brand is split three ways (Google: BNR Skin & Vitiligo Hospital · Practo: Ageless Aesthetics + Vardhan Healthcare · site: Dr Varada's Skin Center). Weeks 1–2, before content:

  1. drvarada.com becomes the umbrella entity — "Dr. Varada's Skin & Hair Center".
  2. One location page per active practice, NAP character-identical to Practo and GBP.
  3. GBP transition naming — "Dr. Varada's Skin & Hair Center (BNR Skin & Vitiligo Hospital)" during transition; a cold rename resets local rank.
  4. Every external profile links home — Practo ×2, GBP, Justdial, Apollo 24/7, Skedoc, ClinicSpots.
  5. Schema everywhere — Organization, Physician, MedicalClinic per location, sameAs → profiles.
Open question (the only one)

Is the Ameerpet / BNR location still operating? Practo lists only West Marredpally + Jubilee Hills. The answer decides whether we run one GBP or two, and whether BNR's vitiligo brand equity gets a location page or a "now at" redirect story.

§2 · Front A

Hyderabad domination

Local intent dwarfs every condition term (Trends: "dermatologist near me" 81 vs the biggest condition cluster 13). The city is won with GBP × location pages × reviews × service pillars.

  • GBP engine. Correct categories (Dermatologist · Skin care clinic · Hair transplantation clinic), weekly posts, photo cadence, seeded Q&A, UTM-tagged links. Review flywheel: QR card at reception → Google (alternating Practo), target +15–20/mo, reply to every one.
  • 15 service pillar pages — one per demand cluster, titles matching autocomplete-verified queries (§4 sitemap). Includes laser hair reduction and dermatosurgery (mole/wart/tag removal) — Practo shows they're offered and they convert.
  • Locality relevance, honestly. Jubilee Hills page carries aesthetics; West Marredpally the morning medical-derm practice. Nearby localities (Banjara Hills, Madhapur, Gachibowli) get mentions and driving directions — not doorway pages.
  • Telugu layer. "psoriasis treatment in telugu" is a real autocomplete with no good clinic content behind it. Telugu versions of the top-6 condition pages, proper hreflang.
  • Citations & local press. Aggregator profiles aligned; Telangana health-desk and IMA Telangana relationships for local authority links.
§3 · Front B

Pan-India vitiligo & psoriasis authority

Vitiligo and psoriasis are journey conditions — chronic, stigmatized, heavily researched. Whoever owns the patient-education journey owns the patient. National competitors have volume but thin template content; we counter with a 41-year specialist's depth.

  • Two national hubs, separate from the local pages:
    /vitiligo/ — types & stages · causes · excimer laser · NB-UVB phototherapy · grafting surgery · children's vitiligo · diet & myths · stability · repigmentation gallery · cost guide · online consultation
    /psoriasis/ — plaque/scalp/guttate/nail types · triggers & flares · phototherapy · systemic & biologic counselling · psoriatic-arthritis red flags · diet · cost guide · online consultation
  • E-E-A-T spine. Every page: "Medically reviewed by Dr. (Lt Col) Khagendra Varada, MBBS, Dip. Practical Dermatology (Cardiff)" with dated review stamps; original photos; consented case stories.
  • National funnel. WhatsApp photo triage → Practo online consultation (he already takes video consults) → plan → travel visit if procedural.
  • Authority assets. People-Also-Ask driven FAQ depth; published repigmentation outcome data (a unique, linkable asset no competitor has); YouTube shorts feeding the hubs; national health-media expertise.
  • Language phases. English → Telugu (phase 1–2) → Hindi hubs in phase 3 — vitiligo demand skews Hindi-belt ("vitiligo treatment in hindi" is a top autocomplete).

§5 · Content system

Cadence, anatomy, compliance

  • Cadence. Phase 1 ships 13 cornerstone pages. Then 2 hub-cluster pages + 2 blog posts per week. Every post internal-links to its pillar; every pillar links to booking + related cluster.
  • T1 page anatomy. Query-matched H1 → credibility line (41 yrs · 97% recommend · locations) → sticky Book on Practo → treatment protocol with named technology → before/after strip → doctor quote → FAQ from autocomplete + People-Also-Ask (FAQPage schema) → Practo stories → related reading.
  • YMYL compliance is strategy. No cure claims (vitiligo copy says repigmentation, control, stability); no invented statistics; consented photos; outcome-variability disclaimers; NMC advertising-ethics compliant. Google punishes medical over-claiming — honesty ranks.
  • Social-proof automation. A scheduled repo script pulls our own Practo profiles (rating, story count, newest stories) → JSON → WordPress, so the "⭐ 5.0 on Practo · 61 stories" strips update themselves. Built as a repo skill (practo_sync).
§6 · Conversion

Practo-first funnel (team directive)

Primary CTA everywhere: Book on Practo — per-location deep link (aesthetics → Ageless Aesthetics · medical derm → morning practice). Secondary: WhatsApp your photos — staff replies with the Practo link; one funnel, one door. Tertiary: tap-to-call. National hub pages lead with Practo online consultation. No parallel booking forms. Direct-booking prominence gets a data-driven test at month 6 — every CTA target is swappable per page without redesign.

§7 · Build spec

Technical development plan

  • Stack: WordPress on Hostinger (installation 24363459), HTTPS ✔, LiteSpeed cache, Elementor available.
  • Build method: design in Open Design → deploy-canvas (direct HTML on elementor_canvas) for templated marketing pages — fast, pixel-faithful, the repo default. Elementor-native only where the clinic team must self-edit. Shared header/footer components.
  • 4 templates (T1–T4) cover all ~45 pages — consistent blocks, consistent schema.
  • Performance gates: LCP < 2.5s on 4G, WebP + lazy-load, page + object cache tuned, Core Web Vitals green before the Phase-2 content push.
  • Schema: Organization · Physician · MedicalClinic per location · MedicalWebPage · FAQPage; hreflang en / te / hi.
  • Analytics: GA4 + Search Console + click tracking on Practo/WhatsApp/call CTAs for per-page lead attribution; rank tracking on the keyword map; monthly report artifact.
  • Housekeeping at build: WP core + plugin updates, delete duplicate AI-builder pages, permalinks, XML sitemap, robots, GSC verification.

§10 · Risks

Known risks & mitigations

  • GBP rename resets local rank → transition naming, never a cold rename.
  • Ameerpet/BNR status unknown → confirm before entity work (§1, the only open question).
  • YMYL / NMC advertising ethics → claim-free, review-stamped physician copy; consented imagery.
  • Practo dependency (owns relationship data, charges for leads) → CTA targets swappable per page; direct-booking test at month 6.
  • Capacity ceiling (300 patients/mo ≈ 12 extra/working day) → pace paid spend against slot headroom; scale online consultations.
